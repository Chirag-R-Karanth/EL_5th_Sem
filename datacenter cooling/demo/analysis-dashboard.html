Key observations

Evidence limitations

  • Previous: 15:00–17:00, door open.
  • Retrofitted: 12:40–14:40, door closed.
  • No synchronized calibrated energy series.
  • 100,000-row telemetry dataset is synthetic.

Research interpretation

These measurements identify a thermal-performance question; they do not establish retrofit causation or energy savings. Repeat both configurations under matched load, door state, ambient conditions and cooling setpoints.
